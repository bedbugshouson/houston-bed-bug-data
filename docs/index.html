What these numbers are, and are not

The counts come from citations under Houston Code of Ordinances Sec. 10-344(a), which covers property conditions that encourage infestation by insects, ectoparasites, rodents or pests. These are code violations, not confirmed bed bug cases. They only capture problems severe or visible enough to be reported and cited, so treat them as a view of where infestation enforcement concentrates, not a full prevalence map.

The citation data covers only the City of Houston. For suburbs outside the city limits, see bed bug heat treatment in Katy and bed bug treatment in Missouri City.

One pattern worth noting

Most of the top ZIP codes have median household incomes below Houston's citywide median. 77009 is the exception: it ranks near the top for citations despite a higher median income, which points to building age and housing density as factors that can matter independently of income.
